The Intelligent Data Analysis and Application Lab (IDAA)

智慧資訊分析與應用實驗室

AI That Works in the Real World

We build and deploy machine learning systems for autonomous drones, marine conservation, blockchain security, and advanced visual recognition. Our research doesn't stop at papers—we ship production code that solves real problems.

What you'll do here:

  • Apply deep learning, reinforcement learning, and computer vision to challenging domains
  • Train drones to fly autonomously and detect marine life underwater
  • Develop AI systems that secure blockchain and recognize complex patterns
